I’m Chris Nelloms. I started Made by Lumi to help small businesses get online, build a presence, and make useful digital services more accessible.

Tell me about your business

Why I’m building this.

Good technology
should open
doors.

I’ve always been interested in AI, and I believe it should be available and accessible to everyone. Small businesses are where I’m starting: helping people establish a presence online and use digital services that fit their work.

Made by Lumi is a one-person business. AI helps me expand what I can create and explore ideas more quickly. What excites me is the range—from a website to a guided invoice web app that uses a business’s own language and visual identity.

The point is to make something useful for the people running the business and the customers they serve.

How I work.

First, I listen.
Then we explore
what’s possible.

I want to understand what you do, what you offer, and what makes your business different. Your ideas are the starting point.

From there, I help bring those ideas to life and show you what’s possible. We look at the direction together, talk honestly about what fits, and agree on what’s worth building.

You don’t need to arrive with a finished plan. A conversation about your business is enough to begin.
